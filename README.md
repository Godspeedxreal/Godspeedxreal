### 1. 📊 Retail Business Performance Dashboard

**[View Repository](https://github.com/Godspeedxreal/Retail-Business-Performance-Dashboard)**

An executive-style retail performance analysis project built with **Microsoft Excel, Power Pivot, DAX, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and interactive slicers**.

**Project highlights**
- Tracks total sales, profit, profit margin, and target achievement.
- Analyzes monthly performance and regional sales.
- Compares product categories and sales channels.
- Reviews sales representative performance and product returns.
- Uses interactive filters for year, region, category, and channel.

**Business value:** Helps stakeholders monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and investigate areas that need attention.

**Tools:** `Excel` `Power Pivot` `DAX` `PivotTables` `PivotCharts`
